# November 3, 2026 East Coast expansion

These five research patches document 67 reviewed entries across NY, ME, NH, MA, RI, CT, NJ, PA, DE, MD, VA, NC, SC, GA and FL. They added 39 records and expanded 28 existing records, preserving existing IDs. The published collection now contains 122 entries, including 35 for New York (30 statewide and 5 NYC).

This is selected coverage, not an exhaustive inventory. New York has the greatest depth. Legal duties, scope and exceptions are separate from editorial practical steps. Entry review dates identify the work performed; untouched entries retain their earlier dates.

## Source limitations and follow-up

- NY S9051B / Chapter 368 (2026): the final bill and official sponsor signing announcement show different future commencement dates. The entry explicitly records that conflict and requires checking the final chapter amendment; it does not present either date as settled.
- Georgia: selected requirements use current official agency explanations alongside an explicitly labeled 2021 Attorney General code compilation. These sources do not certify a complete current-code audit.
- NJ algorithmic discrimination: an existing statutory obligation is explained using official DCR guidance, clearly labeled nonbinding; the direct statutory PDF was not retrievable.
- Connecticut: early 2026 announcements include provisions later repealed or replaced. This patch excludes the unresolved pricing/facial-recognition/subscription additions and distinguishes employment notice operation in October 2027 from section commencement in October 2026.
- Delaware: 2027 amendments are a separate Upcoming entry. Maryland privacy links use the current 14-47xx codification. Maine chatbot legislation links the reallocated §1500-DD.

The `notes` in each patch retain primary URLs and verification details. Tool reference IDs, where present, are research-session locators rather than public citations; the URLs are the durable references. No court decisions, proposals, role questionnaires or separate guidance tabs were added.
