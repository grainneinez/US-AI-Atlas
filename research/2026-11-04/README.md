# Florida expansion — November 4, 2026

Nine new entries and one expanded privacy entry bring Florida from 4 to 13 entries, and the atlas from 122 to 131. Only Florida records and the repository documentation changed. Other states and untouched Florida source-check dates are preserved.

- `research-fl-core.json`: Digital Bill of Rights detail, altered sexual depictions/removal requests, commercial name/likeness use.
- `research-fl-professional.json`: binding lawyer duties, statewide filing representations, patient-record confidentiality.
- `research-fl-general.json`: consumer protection, employment discrimination, fair housing and government records.

Primary statutes, court rules and implementing orders support the legal claims. Advisory ethics material is labeled nonbinding support, never a standalone law. AI examples and practical steps are editorial applications of general law where the text is not AI-specific. Coverage is selected, not exhaustive; federal, local and sector rules may also apply.

## Specific boundaries

Florida's 2026 SB482 and special-session SB2-D did not become law. Their proposed AI Bill of Rights and chatbot provisions were not added. Current §540.08 is the existing likeness statute; no proposed AI amendment is presented as enacted.

Rule 2.515 is included as binding court procedure, not a revived case feed. The accompanying statewide order addresses local AI filing certifications. The Florida Bar compilation's filename begins `2027_04`, but its internal current heading is October 22, 2026; the source date is not inferred from the filename.

The Digital Bill of Rights main threshold does not govern every Florida data obligation. Request, appeal and assessment dates are stated separately. The synthetic-media entry distinguishes conduct offenses from platform-removal duties and retains the statutory exceptions. Employment and housing complaint routes have different coverage and deadlines.

Patch notes retain verification URLs and any session source references; the URLs are the durable citations.
